# What am I paying in fees?

Look at the funds I hold and help me see what they cost.

1. For each fund or ETF, find its expense ratio (say where the number comes from, or that you could not).
2. Turn the ratios into dollars per year on what I hold in each.
3. Add it up. Show what that yearly cost becomes over 20 years at the same balance.
4. Point out any two funds that hold mostly the same things at different prices.

Do not tell me to switch anything. I want to see the cost first.
