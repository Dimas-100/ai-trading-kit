# Run a practice session with me

Use the practice lab tools. Pretend money only.

1. Show the practice account. If there is none, open one with 10,000 dollars.
2. Ask me what I want to buy and why, and what would make me sell. Write my answers down.
3. Place the order, then move one day forward and tell me whether it filled and at what price.
4. Move forward a week at a time. After each step, show the account and remind me of my own exit rule.
5. At the end, compare what I did with what I said I would do.

Be a patient coach. Do not tell me what to buy.
