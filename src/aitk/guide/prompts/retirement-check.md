# Am I on track to retire?

Look at my brokerage and retirement accounts and help me see where I stand. Ask me for anything you
cannot read: my age, when I hope to stop working, and what I add each month.

1. Total what I have across every account you can see.
2. Show how it is split between broad index funds, single stocks, bonds and cash.
3. Using a plain assumption you state (for example 5% a year after inflation), estimate what this could
   grow to by the year I gave you, with and without my monthly additions.
4. Tell me the one number that matters most for reaching my goal: the return, the monthly amount, or
   the time. Show the arithmetic.

Be honest about uncertainty. Estimates, not promises. No product recommendations.
