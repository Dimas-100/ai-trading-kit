# Review my portfolio

Look at my brokerage account and give me a plain-language review.

1. List every position: symbol, shares, current value, and its share of the whole account.
2. Tell me how concentrated I am. What share is in my largest position, and in my largest three?
3. Group the positions by type (single stocks, broad funds, sector funds, bonds, crypto, cash).
4. Point out anything that overlaps, for example two funds that hold mostly the same companies.
5. Tell me how much is sitting in cash.

Show the numbers you used for each statement. If you cannot read something, say so: do not guess.
Do not tell me what to buy or sell. I want to understand what I hold first.
