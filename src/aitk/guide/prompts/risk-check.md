# What could hurt me most

Look at my positions and help me see my risks.

1. If my largest position fell 30%, how much would my whole account fall, in dollars and percent?
2. If the whole stock market fell 20%, roughly what would happen to my account? State the assumption
   you are making.
3. Which positions move together, so that they would likely fall at the same time?
4. Do I have open orders that could fill at a bad moment? Explain each one.

Show your arithmetic. Say clearly which parts are estimates. This is for my understanding, not advice.
