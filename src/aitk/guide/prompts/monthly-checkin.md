# My monthly check-in

Give me a short monthly review of my account, in this order:

1. Total value now, and what changed since last month if you can see it.
2. My three largest positions and what share of the account each is.
3. Anything that moved more than 10% in the month.
4. Open orders, and any position without a protective order under it.
5. Cash sitting idle.

Five short sections. Numbers first, then one sentence each on what it means. No advice on what to
buy or sell; this is a look in the mirror.
