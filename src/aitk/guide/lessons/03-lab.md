# Step 3: Give your AI a practice lab

**What you get:** your AI gains tools to test ideas on price history and to trade a pretend-money
account, so you can say "test this idea for me" in plain words.

**Do it:** `aitk connect-lab`

## What the lab can and cannot do

It can replay a strategy over years of prices, compare strategies, try many settings, and run a
practice account. It works only on pretend money and on price files on your computer. It has no
connection to your broker and cannot reach real money.

## Try saying

- "List the strategies you can test and explain each in one sentence."
- "Backtest sma_cross on SPY and tell me honestly whether it beat buying and holding."
- "Open a practice account with 10,000 dollars and buy 5 shares of SPY."

## Demo prices

Until you download real history, the lab uses made-up prices so everything works at once. Results on
made-up prices teach you the tools, not the market. Step 4 shows how to get real history for free.
