# Step 2: Understand what you own

**What you get:** a set of questions that turn your AI from a chat window into an analyst that has
your real numbers in front of it.

**Do it:** `aitk prompts` to list them, `aitk prompts portfolio-review` to print one. Paste it into
your AI app.

## Why start here

Most people who want to trade better do not need a strategy first. They need to see what they already
hold: how concentrated it is, what it costs, and what would hurt most. A review of your own account
is the cheapest lesson there is.

## Good habits when asking an AI about money

- Ask it to show the numbers it used. If it cannot, it guessed.
- Ask what would make its answer wrong.
- Treat every answer as a starting point for your own thinking. An AI can be confidently mistaken,
  and nothing it says is financial advice.
- Never paste a password or an API key into a chat.
